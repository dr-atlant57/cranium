Why Does My Body Feel Like Part of My Workload?

When the body demands attention while the mind is working, the two workloads become difficult to separate.

Maybe you notice…

CRANIUM explores the physical side of sustained cognitive work.

Look at the context

When does it happen? How long has it been present? What are you doing at the time? Does it change between sitting, standing, moving, working or resting?

The CRANIUM perspective

CRANIUM starts by asking what position the head occupies and how the upper neck supports it. The question is what state we are observing and whether it can be compared with another state.

What we observe

Configuration

Head position, orientation and side-to-side differences.

Context

Workload, movement and duration.

Change

What is different between initial and subsequent observations.

What CRANIUM does

Observe → Calibrate → Identify → Optimize → Verify.

What may change

Changes are documented as observations, measurements or reported experience. CRANIUM does not promise a predetermined result.
