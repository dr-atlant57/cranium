Why Does One Side Feel More Active Than the Other?

A difference between sides can show up in movement, tension or the way the head is held.

Maybe you notice…

CRANIO observes asymmetry without assuming its cause.

Look at the context

When does it happen? How long has it been present? What are you doing at the time? Does it change between sitting, standing, moving, working or resting?

The CRANIO perspective

CRANIO starts by asking what position the head occupies and how the upper neck supports it. The question is what state we are observing and whether it can be compared with another state.

What we observe

Configuration

Head position, orientation and side-to-side differences.

Context

Workload, movement and duration.

Change

What is different between initial and subsequent observations.

What CRANIO does

Observe → Calibrate → Identify → Optimize → Verify.

What may change

Changes are documented as observations, measurements or reported experience. CRANIO does not promise a predetermined result.
