Why Do I Feel Overloaded After Long Workdays?

When sustained intellectual work fills the day, physical effort can become part of the experience of overload.

Maybe you notice…

CRANIUM asks whether the body's support configuration is adding to the total workload.

Look at the context

When does it happen? How long has it been present? What are you doing at the time? Does it change between sitting, standing, moving, working or resting?

The CRANIUM perspective

CRANIUM starts by asking what position the head occupies and how the upper neck supports it. The question is not “what diagnosis is this?” but “what state are we observing, and can we compare it with another state?”

What we observe

Configuration

Head position, orientation and side-to-side differences.

Context

Workload, screen time, concentration, movement and duration.

Change

What is different between initial and subsequent observations.

What CRANIUM does

Observe → Calibrate → Identify → Optimize → Verify. The method creates a sequence that can be inspected instead of relying on a single explanation.

What may change

Changes are documented as observations, measurements or reported experience. CRANIUM does not promise a predetermined result.
