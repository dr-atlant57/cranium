Why Does My Head Always Feel Tense?

Sometimes the experience is less about the neck and more about a head that never seems to settle.

Maybe you notice…

CRANIO starts with the experience, then looks at head position, movement and the upper-neck relationship.

Look at the context

When does it happen? How long has it been present? What are you doing at the time? Does it change between sitting, standing, moving, working or resting?

The CRANIO perspective

CRANIO starts by asking what position the head occupies and how the upper neck supports it. The question is not “what diagnosis is this?” but “what state are we observing, and can we compare it with another state?”

What we observe

Configuration

Head position, orientation and side-to-side differences.

Context

Workload, screen time, concentration, movement and duration.

Change

What is different between initial and subsequent observations.

What CRANIO does

Observe → Calibrate → Identify → Optimize → Verify. The method creates a sequence that can be inspected instead of relying on a single explanation.

What may change

Changes are documented as observations, measurements or reported experience. CRANIO does not promise a predetermined result.
