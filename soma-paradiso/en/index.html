Work, money, family, achievements: from the outside, life may look good. But the things that once excited you no longer feel the same. Even time off may not bring back a sense of ease.

What if life could feel different?

Sometimes a new experience of your physical state can be the start of a turning point. SOMA PARADISO offers individual work focused on physical wellbeing, with a comparison of how you feel before and after. Change is possible; individual outcomes cannot be predicted.

When other support is needed

If you have lost your sense of meaning, experience persistent loss of pleasure or think about death, speak to a mental health professional. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services and someone you trust. The paid programme is not for people in crisis.

Research direction

A separate question for researchers and clinicians: could work with physical wellbeing complement professional care for loss of interest in life, including in discussions of alternatives to assisted dying? This is a proposal for future research, not an active treatment or recruitment programme.

Individual wellness programme

For adults who are not in crisis: head and neck soft-tissue work and a comparison of personal experience before and after. An effect of this method on loss of meaning in life has not been established.
