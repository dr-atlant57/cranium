CRANIO / THE SESSION

What actually happens to your head and neck?

During an in-person visit, a practitioner works on selected muscle areas around the back of the head and neck using a percussion instrument that produces rhythmic mechanical stimulation. We compare perceived comfort and movement before and after.

What to expect

1. A conversation

Discuss what feels uncomfortable, daily workload, sleep, head movement, relevant injury history and signs that need medical assessment.

2. Hands-on work

The practitioner chooses muscle areas and applies a percussion instrument with your informed agreement and according to tolerance. This is physical work, not an online adjustment or a diagnostic test.

3. A comparison

Revisit comfort and movement, and record the observations. A reported difference does not prove changes in bone positions or establish a cause for symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a typical massage?

No. The practice uses a percussion instrument and a structured observation process. This does not establish that it works better than massage or clinical care.

Do I need to buy equipment?

No. This is an in-person practitioner-led session.

What might I feel?

Responses differ. Discomfort or changes in sensations are possible; some people may not perceive a difference. Results are not guaranteed.

How many visits are needed?

A course cannot responsibly be prescribed based only on an online description. Any follow-up is discussed after the initial visit.

Where is it held and what does it cost?

We confirm the exact location, current price and available time through WhatsApp before booking.

When should I seek medical care instead?

Sudden severe headache, weakness or numbness, speech or vision changes, or pain after an injury require urgent medical attention. CRANIO does not diagnose or treat disease. Suitability for hands-on work is assessed individually.
