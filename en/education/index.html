Competence architecture

Observation

Distinguish observed, measured, reported and exploratory concepts.

Configuration

Describe head orientation and context without unsupported diagnosis.

Communication

Agree on needs, scope, safety and voluntary participation.

Practice

Develop practical competencies under supervision, not from website instructions.

Verification

Compare observations under comparable conditions and record changes.

Continuity

Maintain observation history with consent and respect confidentiality.
