CRANIO SYSTEMS · PRACTICAL HEAD & NECK WORK

Is your neck always tense? Does the back of your head feel tight?

CRANIO is an in-person practice. We first discuss what you feel and how your head and neck move, then a practitioner uses a percussion tool on selected muscles around the back of the head and neck, and we compare your observations afterwards. It is a hands-on session, not an app or an online test.

Percussion means rhythmic mechanical stimulation with a dedicated tool. The practice focuses on muscle tension and perceived comfort. Responses vary and no specific outcome is guaranteed.

PRACTICAL WORK, NOT JUST OBSERVATION

What happens when you visit

1. Before

Discuss sensations, daily workload, relevant limitations and signs that require medical assessment. Record starting comfort and movement.

2. During

A practitioner applies individualized percussion to selected muscle areas around the head, occiput and neck, adjusting to your tolerance.

3. After

Compare what you feel and how you move. Discuss observations and possible next steps without promises of a predetermined outcome.

Session details and FAQ

How is CRANIO different from a typical massage?

The practice uses a percussion instrument and a structured before → practice → after observation process. It is not presented as medical treatment, and sensations or posture alone are not used to diagnose disease.

For teams who spend long hours at screens

IT teams, AI professionals and office-based organizations can discuss a voluntary pilot focusing on physical head and neck comfort. Improved productivity is not a proven outcome of this practice and would need separate evaluation.

Discuss a team pilot

Important limitations

CRANIO is a non-medical practice; it does not replace professional assessment or medical treatment. A sudden severe headache, numbness or weakness, changes in speech or vision, or pain after an injury call for urgent medical attention, not a session booking. Suitability and relevant precautions are discussed before any hands-on work.
