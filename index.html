11 / Questions
What is CRANIUM?

CRANIUM is a head and neck optimization system centered on the relationship between the skull, the upper neck and the way the head is positioned and moves.

Why do you start with the head?

The head is central to orientation, vision, communication and movement. CRANIUM starts by establishing a clearer head–upper-neck reference before interpreting downstream patterns.

What is C0–C1?

C0 refers to the occipital part of the skull and C1 to the atlas, the first cervical vertebra. Their relationship is a primary reference in the CRANIUM model.

Why percussion?

Controlled percussion is used as a practical method for working with tension around the head and neck before focusing on the head–upper-neck relationship.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. CRANIUM's public language is intentionally focused on observation, human experience and optimization rather than diagnosing disease.

What is TwinMind?

TwinMind is the cognitive continuity layer designed to remember context, observations, states and changes across time.
