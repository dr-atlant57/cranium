10 / Questions
What is CRANIUM?

CRANIUM is a head and neck optimization system built around the relationship between the skull, the upper neck and the way the head is positioned.

Why start with the head?

Because the head is the reference from which we experience the world: seeing, speaking, orienting, concentrating and moving. CRANIUM starts there instead of beginning with isolated downstream complaints.

What is C0–C1?

C0 is the occipital part of the skull and C1 is the atlas, the first cervical vertebra. CRANIUM uses their relationship as a primary reference for its work.

Why percussion?

Controlled percussion is used as a practical way to work with tension around the head and neck before addressing the head–upper-neck reference.

Do I need to understand the science?

No. The deeper architecture exists to make the system coherent. The public experience is deliberately explained in ordinary human language.
