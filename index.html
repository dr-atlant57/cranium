01 / Principle

Find the upstream reference.

The CRANIUM model begins with geometry: reference axes, spatial relationships, load distribution, movement and compensation. The objective is not to label a symptom. It is to understand the configuration from which observable patterns emerge.

CRANIUM separates observation, hypothesis, interpretation and verification. A model is not presented as established fact merely because it is useful.

03 / Primordocciput

A hypothesis, not a doctrine.

The Primordocciput hypothesis proposes that a primary configuration at the cranio-cervical junction may participate in a cascade of compensations through the body.

This is a research model. CRANIUM keeps the distinction between a proposed mechanism and independently verified evidence explicit.

05 / Method

Observe. Map. Calibrate. Verify.

A repeatable workflow keeps the architecture measurable and prevents interpretation from outrunning observation.

Observe

Capture the current configuration without prematurely naming its cause.

Map

Represent axes, vectors, relationships and relevant downstream observations.

Calibrate

Apply the defined structural intervention or configuration change.

Verify

Compare the resulting state against the initial reference and record what actually changed.

06 / Evidence

Keep epistemic layers separate.

OBSERVATION

What was seen

Directly recorded geometry, movement, measurements or user-reported experience.

HYPOTHESIS

What may explain it

A proposed relationship that remains open to testing and revision.

VERIFICATION

What changed

Repeatable before/after comparison and longitudinal tracking.

08 / Philosophy

The goal is not to force the body into a template. The goal is to reveal the architecture, establish a reference, and observe what changes when the reference changes.

09 / FAQ
Is CRANIUM a medical system?

CRANIUM is presented here as a structural and observational framework, not as a diagnostic or medical-treatment system.

What is the role of C0–C1?

Within the CRANIUM working model, C0–C1 is used as an upstream reference for investigating cranio-cervical geometry. The causal interpretation remains a hypothesis where evidence is incomplete.

What is TwinMind?

TwinMind is the planned cognitive layer that maintains a longitudinal representation of observations, states, changes and relationships within the CRANIUM architecture.

Why separate observation from interpretation?

Because a useful model must remain auditable. What was observed, what was inferred and what was verified should never silently become the same thing.
