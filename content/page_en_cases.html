Themes in individual observations

These are archive themes, not three independently verified clinical cases. Every future public record will require source materials and appropriate consent.

Neck tension

Individual reports of a change in perceived tension and comfort after soft-tissue work.

Head mobility

Observations of differences in ease of movement that require comparable pre/post measurements.

Visible symmetry

Photographic observations requiring consistent framing, lighting and independent review.

What a case record must contain

Baseline

Participant's own description and date.

Intervention

Type of work, session count and limitations.

Outcomes

Measurements, observations and participant reports separately.

Follow-up

Later observations, including adverse effects.

Historical marketing numbers without original measurements are not presented as verified evidence. Session counts are not the same as validated case counts.

Safety and limitations

CRANIO is a nonmedical practice, not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment. Sudden severe headache, numbness, weakness, speech or vision disturbances or pain after injury require urgent medical assessment. External observations do not prove C0–C1 bone displacement, and outcomes are not guaranteed.
